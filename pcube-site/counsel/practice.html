Property and land

Buying, selling, inheriting or defending property is often the largest decision a family makes. We check the details before you commit, and stand with you if a dispute arises.

Practice areas Civil disputes Criminal cases Family matters Property and land Business and contracts Consumer complaints

How we help

  • Title verification and a clear report before you buy
  • Drafting and registration of sale, gift and settlement deeds
  • Partition of family property, by agreement or through court
  • Boundary, possession and encroachment disputes
  • Tenancy and eviction matters for owners and tenants

Situations we often see

Buying a plot or flatYou want to be sure the seller owns it outright and there are no hidden claims.
Dividing family landHeirs need a fair, legally sound partition, ideally without going to court.
A neighbour's encroachmentA wall, path or structure has crossed onto your land.
A tenant who won't leaveRent is unpaid or the agreement has ended and possession is disputed.

Documents to bring

Title deeds and prior deeds, tax receipts, encumbrance certificate, possession and location certificates, survey sketch, and any notices or agreements you have received. If you don't have all of them, come anyway. We will tell you what is missing.
